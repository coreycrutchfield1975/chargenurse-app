# ChargeNurse v5.3 Test Checklist

1. Open `veterans.html`.
2. Add a Veteran with name and room.
3. Confirm the Veteran appears in Scheduler.
4. Create an appointment for the Veteran.
5. Open the Veteran record and confirm the appointment appears.
6. Use **Open in Scheduler** and verify the correct appointment opens.
7. Change the Veteran's room and name.
8. Confirm existing appointment records display the updated name and room.
9. Add treatment schedule days and confirm they persist after refresh.
10. Archive the Veteran and confirm the record remains under Archived.
11. Confirm archived Veterans do not appear in Scheduler's active Veteran selector.
12. Reactivate the Veteran.
13. Search by name, room, provider, and last four.
14. Confirm there are no photo, primary nurse, Hermes, or AI fields.
15. Export a backup before production deployment.
