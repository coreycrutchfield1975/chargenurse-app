2. Select a Veteran and enter an appointment date.
3. Enter destination, clinic, Provider, phone, fax, transportation, and escort.
4. Click Print Ticket to Ride before saving.
5. Confirm the print sheet contains the entered information.
6. Confirm wheelchair and oxygen needs display correctly.
7. Save the appointment.
8. Use Print Ticket to Ride from the daily agenda.
9. Confirm the saved appointment information appears.
10. Confirm printing does not automatically check the Ticket to Ride status.
11. Confirm the Travel Request safeguard still appears when transportation is required.
12. Confirm No Transportation Needed still saves without transportation.
13. Confirm Day, Week, Month, and Year views remain operational.
14. Confirm Provider defaults and editable master lists still work.
