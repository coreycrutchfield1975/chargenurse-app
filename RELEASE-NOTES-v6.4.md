# ChargeNurse v6.4 — Printable Ticket to Ride

Phase 16 adds a dedicated printable Ticket to Ride while preserving the existing transportation workflow.

## Scheduler
A Ticket to Ride can now be printed:
- Directly from the appointment editor
- From a saved appointment in the daily agenda

The ticket uses the current appointment information, including unsaved information entered in the editor.

## Ticket contents
- Veteran and room
- Appointment date and time
- Requested departure time
- Destination and clinic
- Provider, phone, and fax
- Escort and transportation method
- Transportation email
- Appointment reason and notes
- Wheelchair and oxygen needs
- Travel Request status
- Transportation confirmation, departure, and return checkboxes
- Driver/escort and nursing signature lines
- Actual departure and return time lines

Printing does not automatically mark the Ticket to Ride checkbox. Staff remain responsible for confirming that the ticket was prepared.

## Preserved
- Scheduler ownership of appointments and transportation
- Travel Request email safeguard
- Ticket to Ride status checkbox
- Day, Week, Month, and Year views
- Provider defaults and shared master lists
