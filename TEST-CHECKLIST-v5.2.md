# ChargeNurse v5.2 Appearance Test Checklist

1. Open Command Center and select Appearance.
2. Test Page Default.
3. Test at least two built-in backgrounds.
4. Change overlay, panel opacity, fit, and position.
5. Confirm the same choice appears in Scheduler.
6. Confirm the same choice appears in Morning Report, Treatment Sheets, and RN Workstation.
7. Place a test JPG in `backgrounds/custom/`.
8. Enter its relative path in Appearance and select Use Project Image.
9. Upload a personal JPG, PNG, or WEBP and confirm it remains after refresh.
10. Select Reset to Page Default.
11. Confirm printing does not show the Appearance button or dialog.
12. Confirm no background image contains PHI.
