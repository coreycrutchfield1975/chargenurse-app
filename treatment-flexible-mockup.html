Key Features:

  • Flexible 14-day range - Select any starting day (e.g., Day 5-18, Day 12-25)
  • Running 2-week sheet - Update the range as needed for continuity
  • PRN shift included - All three shifts (Day, PRN, Night) have boxes
  • Last 4 digits - Included in patient header
  • Category-specific styling - Licensed (purple) vs Non-Licensed (green)
  • No signature line - Initials in boxes serve as documentation
