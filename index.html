📖 First Time Setup

Step 1: Add your Veterans, Appointments, and Treatments — everything saves automatically in your browser.

Step 2: Once you have data, click "Set Auto-Save Folder" above and pick your S: drive folder. This saves a backup file that other nurses can use.

Step 3 (next time you open the app): A gold banner will appear on the Dashboard — click "Reconnect" and pick the same folder. Your data loads from the S: drive file.

Tip: If you're opening on a new computer, go to Import Data first and select the bravoshift-data.json file from your S: drive, then set up auto-save.
