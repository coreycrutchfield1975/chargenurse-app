📋 Interview Selection Process

📋 Panel Instructions

1. The Interview Panel Leader will determine how the team will function. Panel will repeat or clarify as requested.

2. Write the applicant's name, rate responses 1-5, and add brief comments to support each rating.

3. At the end of each interview, tally the scores, total, sign and date.

4. The Panel Leader will complete the recommendations with signatures and return to the selecting official.

5. Do not discuss candidates except with appropriate management.
