📋 Interview Selection Process

📋 Panel Instructions (reference — read-only)

1. The Interview Panel Leader will determine how the team will function (one person ask all questions or round robin). Panel will repeat and/or provide clarification as requested by the applicant.

2. At the beginning of each interview, Panel Members should write the applicant's name at the top of the interview questions and rate the applicant's responses to each question using a scale of 1-5 with "1" being little or no experience and "5" being extensive experience. Brief comments should be written to support their rating decision.

3. At the conclusion of each interview, Panel Members will tally the scores for each of the questions/items and will document the total score in the space provided and sign and date the document.

4. At the conclusion of the interview process, the Interview Panel Leader should:

  • Complete the Interview Panel Recommendations, including signatures from each of the Interview Panel Members.
  • Return all documents, including Interview Panel Recommendations, to the selecting official.

5. Members of the panel should not discuss who qualified for the position, an interview, or who the selection is except with the appropriate management official.

📋 Candidate Information

🎤 Interview Introduction (read to each candidate — editable)

🗣️ Current Poplar Bluff Employees Only

If you currently work at the John J. Pershing VA Medical Center, please be aware that the panel members are prohibited from using any personal knowledge they may have about you or your work history during the interview process. Instead, they will rate your responses to each individual question. Therefore, it is imperative that you elaborate on your responses to provide a complete picture of your knowledge, experience and qualifications for the position for which you are applying.

If you are a bargaining unit member, you have a right to have union representation during this interview.
