# ChargeNurse v6.6 — A More Human Command Center

Phase 18 gives the Command Center more personality without changing nursing workflows.

## Fun shift greetings
The standard Good morning / afternoon / evening greeting has been replaced with rotating, shift-appropriate greetings.

Examples include:
- Coffee checked. Census checked. Chaos, please take a number.
- The afternoon plot twist has entered the building.
- The sun clocked out. Nursing did not.

The signed-in staff name remains part of the greeting.

## Nursing Spark
A new card appears beneath the Command Center introduction. It rotates through:
- Brief nursing-history information
- Original nursing encouragement
- Light workplace humor
- Reminders about teamwork, handoffs, documentation, and coordination

A staff member can click Another one to display the next message.

## Preserved
- Command Center workflow and metrics
- Scheduler and transportation ownership
- Morning Report links
- Veteran Records links
- Printing
- Existing shared data
