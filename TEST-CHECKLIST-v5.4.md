# ChargeNurse v5.4 Operations Calendar Test Checklist

1. Enter shower, weight, and vital days through the existing admission/Veteran modal.
2. Open `operations-calendar.html`.
3. Confirm Today shows only Veterans due on the selected day.
4. Confirm the room and Veteran name are shown.
5. Move to the previous and next day.
6. Select Week.
7. Confirm each day displays counts for showers, weights, vitals, and appointments.
8. Select a day and confirm the exact Veteran list appears.
9. Create an appointment in Scheduler and confirm it appears on the correct calendar day.
10. Confirm the Operations Calendar has no edit, assignment, completion, note, or documentation controls.
11. Print the Today and Week views.
12. Confirm all existing modules still open normally.
