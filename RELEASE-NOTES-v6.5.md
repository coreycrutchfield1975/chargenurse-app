# ChargeNurse v6.5 — Transportation Department Defaults

Phase 17 adds editable transportation department contact defaults.

## Settings
The Transportation tab now stores:
- Transportation email
- Transportation phone
- Transportation fax
- Standing transportation instructions

## Scheduler
New appointments can automatically fill these values when the fields are blank. Staff may edit them for an individual appointment.

## Travel Request
The Travel Request now includes:
- Transportation phone
- Transportation fax
- Standing transportation instructions

The configured transportation email is used as the default recipient.

## Ticket to Ride
The printable ticket now includes the transportation department contact details and standing instructions.

## Historical accuracy
Transportation contact information is stored with each appointment so later Settings changes do not alter older records.
