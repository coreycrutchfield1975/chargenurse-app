# ChargeNurse v5.4 — Operations Calendar

## Added
- Read-only Operations Calendar for showers, weights, vitals, and appointments.
- Today view showing exactly who is due.
- Week view showing daily counts.
- Clickable weekly days showing the names and rooms due that day.
- Previous, next, and today navigation.
- Print support.
- Navigation links from the existing operational modules.

## Scope preserved
- The calendar uses schedules already entered in the existing admission/Veteran modal.
- No duplicate schedule entry.
- No staff assignments.
- No completion documentation.
- No comments, timestamps, clinical assessments, or new task fields.
- No AI integration.
