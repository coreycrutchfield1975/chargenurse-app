# ChargeNurse v5.6 Scheduler Test Checklist

1. Open Scheduler and create an appointment.
2. Leave Transportation needed checked.
3. Click Save Appointment.
4. Confirm the Travel Request popup appears before the appointment is saved.
5. Confirm the popup includes Veteran, room, date, times, location, clinic, provider, escort, transportation, wheelchair, oxygen, and reason.
6. Enter the correct transportation email.
7. Click Open Travel Request Email and confirm the default email application opens.
8. Return to ChargeNurse and confirm the final save button becomes available.
9. Save and confirm Travel Request sent is stored.
10. Edit the appointment and confirm Ticket to Ride remains available and independent.
11. Create another appointment, choose No Transportation Needed, and confirm the appointment saves without a Travel Request.
12. Confirm cancelling transportation does not delete or cancel the appointment.
13. Confirm confirmation, departure, returned/completed, not completed, chart flagged, and return time still save correctly.
14. Confirm Scheduler printing still works.
15. Confirm Command Center, Morning Report, Operations Calendar, and Veteran pages still open.
