# ChargeNurse v5.3 — Shared Veteran Roster

## Added
- Dedicated `veterans.html` workspace.
- One Veteran record shared with Scheduler and Command Center.
- Add and edit Veteran records.
- Archive and reactivate records without deleting history.
- Search by name, preferred name, room, provider, program, or last four.
- Overview, appointments, and treatment schedule tabs.
- Rule-based risk flags for falls, skin, isolation, and behavior precautions.
- Appointment deep links from the Veteran record into Scheduler.
- Automatic synchronization of Veteran name and room changes to existing appointments.

## Deliberately excluded
- No Veteran photographs.
- No primary nurse field.
- No Hermes, CubiCorey-AI, voice assistant, AI-generated alerts, or external AI connection.
- ChargeNurse remains a local, deterministic nursing operations application.
