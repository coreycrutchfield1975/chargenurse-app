# ChargeNurse v5.9 — Scheduler Calendar Views

Scheduler now includes Day, Week, Month, and Year views.

Previous and Next adjust to the active view. Today returns to the current date. The selected view is remembered locally. Selecting a date in Year view opens Day view, and selecting a month title opens Month view.

The existing appointment modal, Travel Request email safeguard, Ticket to Ride, transportation workflow, daily agenda, printing, and deep links remain intact.
