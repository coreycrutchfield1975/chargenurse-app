# ChargeNurse v6.5 Test Checklist

1. Open Settings and select Transportation.
2. Enter transportation email, phone, fax, and standing instructions.
3. Save the transportation defaults.
4. Open a new Scheduler appointment.
5. Confirm blank transportation contact fields fill automatically.
6. Confirm staff can edit the values for one appointment.
7. Start the Travel Request workflow.
8. Confirm the configured email is the default recipient.
9. Confirm the email preview includes phone, fax, and standing instructions.
10. Print the Ticket to Ride.
11. Confirm transportation contact details appear.
12. Save and reopen the appointment.
13. Confirm the values remain stored.
14. Change Settings and confirm the older appointment retains its saved values.
15. Confirm the existing transportation workflow and calendar views remain operational.
