# ChargeNurse v5.6 — Protected Scheduler Workflow

## Preserved
- Existing appointment calendar and agenda
- Ticket to Ride tracking
- Travel Request status
- Confirmation, departure, return/completion, not completed, and chart flag workflow
- Scheduler ownership of appointment editing
- Existing print support and shared Veteran records

## Restored and strengthened
- Transportation email field in the appointment modal
- Travel Request preparation/reason
- Wheelchair and oxygen indicators
- Travel Request email popup before saving an appointment that requires transportation
- Email draft includes the appointment and transportation details
- The appointment cannot be saved as transportation-required until the email draft has been opened
- Staff may cancel the transportation workflow and save the appointment as not requiring transportation
- The transportation email address is remembered locally after first use

## Scope
This phase preserves the working scheduler logic. Changes are limited to connecting the original transportation fields, protecting the Travel Request step, and maintaining the current visual design.
